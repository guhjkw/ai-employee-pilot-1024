For a founder building a team of AI employees across recruiting, creator agency work and operations

An AI employee that does the sourcing work, and asks you only about the judgement calls.

This is a working preview, not a video. The recruiting employee screens every candidate against the role brief and shows the evidence. Anything that is a judgement call (salary stretch, relocation, unknown work status) goes to you with what you decided last time. Approved candidates move to a second agent that schedules and drafts outreach for your approval. Below it, the integration rule decides how an AI employee should reach each app you use, and when reverse-engineering is the right tool and when it is not.

        How the pilot works

        Four steps, one of them yours.

        1. YouWalk me through the apps and the three workflows that take the most of your week.
        2. MeMap every app to a route (MCP, API, export, browser, or a licensed reverse-engineering study) and propose the architecture.
        3. MeBuild one AI employee end to end in Claude Code: tools, approval queue in Slack, shared memory, and a test set of real cases.
        4. MeRun it on live work for a week, tune it, then plan the next employees on the same foundation.
